## 1. Scale check (image 1, displayed 1500x2000 px; multiply by 2 for the original)

Rulers: C&H Powdered Sugar 1 lb carton standing on the ball between the feet, and the two 3 in Post-it notes on the chest.
I could not find published outside dimensions for the C&H 1 lb carton (searched: [chsugar.com packaging equivalents](https://chsugar.com/baking-tips-how-tos/chr-sugar-packaging-equivalents), [thedonutwhole](https://www.thedonutwhole.com/what-size-box-does-powdered-sugar-come-in/), [Target listing](https://www.target.com/p/-/A-13379073) — none list L x W x H; only the 4 lb bag is listed at 14.0 x 8.3 x 2.2 in on [desertcart](https://www.desertcart.ae/products/21376396-powdered-sugar-4-lbs-1.md)).

## 7. Questions for Tony — ranked by how much the answer changes the tip / resonance result

1. **Where does the bottom of the post go?** Into the cart deck (screwed), into the pole's original steel base plate (and is the plate screwed or only velcro'd to the deck/cradle), or into nothing (the "gap")? This alone moves the tip threshold between 5.3 m/s2 (fixed) and ~1.1 m/s2 (pinned with play) and decides whether the ball shell is structural.
2. **What is the "gap" in the sketch** — between ball bottom and deck, or between post bottom and deck — and how big (mm)?
3. **Is the wing nut the pole's telescoping height lock** (two tubes sliding) or a clamp bolting the post to the shell? Is the joint tight when you twist the bear by the shoulders (any click/play)?
4. **Slot clearance**: can you rock the post in the slot by hand? How many mm across, and does the post touch an end of the slot, or is it free ~10 cm along it?
5. **Ball shell**: what is it made of and how thick is the wall (knock on it; look through the slot)? Is anything inside (wood block, foam, the pole's base plate)? What does the ball weigh (lift it)?
6. **Velcro**: size of the patches at the feet and at the ball bottom, hook-and-loop or adhesive strips, and does the ball sit in a wooden cradle/ring (what diameter of contact) or flat on the carpet?
7. **Sugar box outside dimensions** (W x D x H, tape measure) — it fixes the ball diameter (0.44-0.50 m?) and bear height (1.1-1.3 m?) that replace the old 0.65 m / 1.78 m guesses.
8. **Does the dance mechanism move the torso relative to the post** (post fixed, torso swings) or the whole bear above the hips? And how heavy is the bear without pole and plate (bathroom scale)?
